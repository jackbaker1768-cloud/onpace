Why don’t I have a verdict yet?

On Pace waits for about 3 weeks of weigh-ins (week 1 is left out, because it’s mostly water) so the verdict is reliable. Home shows how many days are left.

Why does my verdict say “too fast” when I’m on a cut?

On a cut, “too fast” means you’re losing faster than your plan. The nudge will be to eat a little more, to protect your muscle.

How do I switch from a bulk to a cut?

Settings › Your plan › Change. Your weigh-ins and past plans are kept.

Does On Pace work with Apple Health?

Yes, if you turn it on (during setup or in Settings › Apple Health). Weigh-ins from your smart scale or other apps come in each time you open On Pace, and the ones you log are saved to Health. If nothing comes in, open the Health app, tap your profile picture, then Apps › On Pace, and turn on Weight.

How do I get a reminder to weigh in?

Settings › Reminder › Daily reminder, then pick a time. There’s no reminder on days you’ve already weighed in. If it never arrives, check iPhone Settings › Notifications › On Pace.

How do I move my data?
