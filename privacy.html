On Pace

Privacy Policy

Last updated 15 November 2026

On Pace doesn’t collect any personal data.

  • No account or sign-up.
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising. On Pace doesn’t connect to any server.
  • Your weigh-ins and plans are stored only on your iPhone. We can’t see them. Like your other apps’ data, they’re included in your iPhone’s own iCloud or computer backup if you’ve turned that on.
  • If you export a CSV file or share a progress card, it goes only where you choose to send it. If you import a CSV file, On Pace reads only the file you pick.
  • If you save a progress card to Photos, On Pace asks for permission to add to your photo library. It never reads your photos.
  • If you turn on the daily reminder, it’s set up on your iPhone. No server is involved.

Apple Health

  • If you turn on Apple Health, On Pace reads and writes your body weight in Health, and nothing else.
  • On Pace never sends your Health data anywhere or stores it in iCloud, and never shares, sells or uses it for advertising or marketing.
  • You can stop access any time: turn off Apple Health in On Pace’s Settings, or in the Health app (profile picture › Apps › On Pace).

Deleting your data

  • Settings › Delete all data, or deleting the app, permanently removes everything On Pace stored on your iPhone.
  • Weigh-ins On Pace saved to Apple Health stay in Health until you delete them there (Health app › Browse › Body Measurements › Weight).

If this policy changes, this page and its date will be updated.
