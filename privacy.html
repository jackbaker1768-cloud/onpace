Privacy Policy

Last updated 9 November 2026

On Pace doesn’t collect any personal data.

  • No account or sign-up.
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • Your weigh-ins and plans are stored only on your iPhone. We can’t see them.
  • If you export a CSV file or share a progress card, it goes only where you choose to send it.
  • If you save a progress card to Photos, On Pace asks for permission to add to your photo library. It never reads your photos.
  • If you turn on Apple Health, On Pace reads and writes your body weight in Health, and nothing else. That data stays on your iPhone and is never shared, sold or used for advertising.
  • If you turn on the daily reminder, it’s set up on your iPhone. No server is involved.
  • Deleting the app, or using Settings › Delete all data, removes everything permanently.
